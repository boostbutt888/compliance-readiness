Roadblocks we clear

Don't pay for what you don't need.

Without the right know-how, compliance projects drift: the wrong standard, too much scope, tools nobody asked for, audits failed for missing records. We've seen these before, so you don't have to learn them the expensive way.

Chasing the wrong standard

Buying tools you don't need

Missing the deadline

Where your money goes

Parang charges for consulting. Audits are paid to independent bodies.

Certification only counts if the auditor is independent. International rules (ISO/IEC 17021-1) stop certification bodies from consulting for the companies they certify, so the firm that helps you prepare can never be the firm that certifies you. Every report from the check shows the two separately.

Straight answers

What we won't promise.

A sharp plan starts with realistic expectations. These are the things clients most often wish they'd known earlier.

Certificates can't be rushed

Auditors need to see your controls running, usually for at least 3 months. Working harder doesn't move the audit date.

Audit fees don't go to us

You pay the certification body or auditor directly, and they must be independent of us. We show their likely fees up front so the total isn't a surprise.

Your team still has work to do

We do the drafting, but someone inside needs a few hours a week to make decisions and gather evidence. The report tells you roughly how many.

Self-assessments are optimistic

Most people rate themselves higher than an auditor would. We check a sample of your answers before quoting.

There are costs after year one

Surveillance audits, renewals and tools carry on. We list them alongside the first-year figure.

A certificate isn't a guarantee

It shows you manage risk properly. It doesn't mean nothing will ever go wrong.

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Is the check really free?

Yes. The check, the report and the downloads are free, with no sign-up. You only talk to us if you choose to send your report.

What happens to my answers?

They're saved in your own browser on this device. Nothing is sent to us unless you fill in the contact form and choose to send it.

How much does Parang charge?

It depends on your scope, so we don't publish a price list. After the free 30-minute review we give you a competitive fixed-fee proposal, sized to what you actually need and your budget, with no obligation.

Are the audit and certification fees paid to Parang?

No. Parang only charges for consulting. Certification bodies, audit firms, assessors, rating providers and tool vendors are independent of us, and you pay them directly. Each report shows Parang's fees and third-party fees on separate lines.

How accurate are the cost and time estimates?

They're indicative, based on your company size and how ready you say you are. Auditor and tool prices vary by provider, and we'll help you compare real quotes.

Do I need to be technical?

No. The questions are in plain language. If you're not sure, answer "Partly" and we'll check it together.

We only need one certificate. Is this overkill?

Use the Direct route: pick the one you need, answer a few scoping questions, and skip straight to a quote if you like.

A customer gave us a deadline that seems impossible.

Use the Deadline route. It shows whether the deadline is realistic and what interim evidence buyers usually accept, such as a CSA mark, a SOC 2 Type I report or a signed project plan.
