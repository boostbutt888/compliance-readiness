# Readiness Check: framework scope, client flows and cost model

Decided 18 Nov 2026. The self-assessment app covers 21 frameworks.

## Frameworks in scope
- Cybersecurity: ISO 27001, CSA Cyber Essentials, CSA Cyber Trust, SOC 2 Type II, PCI DSS v4.0.1, NIST CSF 2.0 / CIS v8
- Privacy: PDPA + DPTM, EU GDPR, ISO 27701:2025
- Resilience and availability: ISO 22301, MAS TRM + BCM, ISO 20000-1
- AI and cloud: ISO 42001 + AI Verify, MTCS SS 584 / ISO 27017/27018
- Sector: Healthcare (HCSA / MOH), Government vendor (IM8 / ICT&SS)
- Sustainability: GHG Protocol / ISO 14064-1 carbon footprint, ISO 14001, ISO 50001, SGX/ACRA ISSB climate reporting, EcoVadis

## Client entry routes
1. Guided: "I'm not sure where to start". Goals → Business → Your plan → Quick check → Report.
   - The client picks plain-language goals, a budget band, a deadline and who can lead internally.
   - The plan sorts frameworks into "Start here" (required ones first, then one framework per goal within budget, up to 3), "Next" and "Later".
   - Each plan card shows a plain explanation, the time, the first-year cost and the deadline fit.
2. Deadline: "A customer, tender or regulator is asking". The request → Business → Deadline check → Practices → Report.
   - The client records who asked, what they asked for, the deadline and what happens if they miss it.
   - The deadline check rates each request as Fits, Tight or Won't fit, gives the fastest credible route (interim options), and flags anything else the law requires.
   - Requested frameworks are labelled "Requested" and treated as must-haves.
3. Direct: "I know which framework I need". Choose → Scoping (only the questions those frameworks need) → Self-check → Report.
   - The client can skip the self-check and request a quote; estimates then assume "Partly" on every practice.
4. Full check: screens all 21 frameworks.
- Clients can switch routes at any time and keep their answers.

## Expectation management built into every report
1. "Your situation": budget fit, deadline fit for each framework, whether an internal lead is assigned, and a note when the self-check was skipped.
2. "What you're getting into" for each framework type: the journey stages with durations, what's paid separately, the yearly costs after, and options if time is short.
3. "How working with us runs":
   - The five engagement steps: free review → evidence check → fixed-fee proposal → delivery → audit support.
   - What we need from the client, including internal hours a week.
   - What the client gets, what's not included, and common surprises.
4. The consultant brief adds "Their situation and expectations": the route taken, goals, the request details, and flags to raise on the call (budget, deadline, internal lead, scope, skipped check).

## Cost model assumptions (indicative, need calibrating against real quotes)
- Consultant day rate default S$1,200, set in Firm settings. Fee range −10% to +15%.
- Days per framework = set-up days by headcount band + gap days (S=1, M=2, L=4; halved for "Partly"; scaled 0.8–1.8 by size; lighter for Cyber Essentials, NIST, EcoVadis, PDPA, GDPR, Gov vendor, Healthcare).
- ISO certification body: ISO/IEC 27006 audit-day bands (5 to 17 days) at S$1,300–1,800 a day. Other standards use a fraction of that.
- SOC 2 Type II audit S$30,000–70,000 (scaled); PCI DSS Report on Compliance S$30,000–90,000, or S$3,000–10,000 for self-assessment support; Cyber Essentials S$1,000–3,000; Cyber Trust S$3,000–10,000; DPTM S$4,000–10,000. All scaled by size.
- Internal client time = 1.5 × consultant days, shown as hours a week over the programme.

## Facts used (checked Nov 2026)
- SGX/ACRA (25 Aug 2025): Scope 1 and 2 mandatory for all listed issuers from FY2025; Scope 3 from FY2026 for STI constituents; other ISSB disclosures FY2025 (STI), FY2028 (non-STI with market cap of S$1bn or more), FY2030 (others); external limited assurance deferred to FY2029; large non-listed companies deferred to FY2030.
- CSA expanded Cyber Essentials and Cyber Trust to cloud, AI and OT (15 Apr 2025). CISO-as-a-Service co-funds up to 70% of cyber consultancy for eligible SMEs.
- MAS: notify within 1 hour of discovering a relevant incident; root-cause report within 14 days.
- ISO/IEC 27701:2025 can be certified on its own.
- Enterprise Singapore: PSG carbon management solutions and the SME Sustainability Hub (May 2025).
